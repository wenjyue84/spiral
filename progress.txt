**Implementation Approach:**
1.  **`build_dag(root_prd_path)`:**
    *   Use a queue (BFS) or recursion (DFS) to traverse the `prd.include` paths.
    *   Read each `prd.json`, parse it, and resolve the relative `include` paths to absolute paths.
    *   Use a `visited` set to avoid redundant processing.
